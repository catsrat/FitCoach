Your photos and data

Your photos, meals, and check ins are stored on the server that runs this app and only you can open them. Each photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the estimate or feedback. Messages to your coach are stored too, and are sent to the same API with a summary of your plan, meals, and check ins. Removing a meal or check in deletes its photo, and deleting your account erases everything.
